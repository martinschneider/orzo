| M        | `MethodCallParser` / static-import resolution      | A bare (unqualified) call to a statically-imported **varargs** method (e.g. `import static ...FactoryHelper.list; ...; list(AccessFlag.ACC_PUBLIC, AccessFlag.ACC_STATIC)`) fails to resolve ("missing method declaration list[AccessFlag,AccessFlag,AccessFlag]"), even though `FactoryHelper.list(E... e)` exists. Kept in `skiplist.txt`. |
